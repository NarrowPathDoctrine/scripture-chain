About Scripture Chain

Scripture Chain helps readers follow connected passages of the Bible through carefully curated studies. Rather than isolating a verse, each chain moves from passage to passage so Scripture can be compared with Scripture.

Study throughout the year

  • 365 Daily Chains for year-round reading
  • Topical studies and studies by need
  • KJV-focused Bible study experience
  • Saved progress for returning to your studies
  • Completely free and ad free
  • No account required
