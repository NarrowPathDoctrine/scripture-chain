Scripture Chain

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Scripture Chain is provided by Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries. Scripture Chain is designed to function primarily on your device and does not require an account to use.

Information we collect

Scripture Chain does not collect, transmit, or share personal information such as your name, email address, precise location, contacts, payment information, or advertising identifiers.

Information stored on your device

The app stores certain information locally on your device so its features can work properly. This may include saved study progress, saved content, Daily Chain reminder settings, and your selected reminder time. This information is not transmitted to Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries by the app.

Notifications

If you choose to enable the Daily Chain reminder, Scripture Chain may request permission to display notifications. Reminder notifications are scheduled and generated locally on your device. You can disable the reminder at any time in the app's Settings screen or change notification permission through Android settings.

Advertising and analytics

Scripture Chain is ad free. The app does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

External links

Scripture Chain may provide links that open third-party websites or services, including YouTube and Buy Me a Coffee. Once you leave Scripture Chain, those services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Scripture Chain does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Scripture Chain's features or privacy practices change. The current version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the developer contact information provided on Scripture Chain's Google Play listing.
